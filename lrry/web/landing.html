L/RRY

Stereo-Field
Morphing Synthesizer
by OSCILL/R

Every sound moves around you. L/RRY is free, open-source firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, from the creator of DUELL. Each note gets two oscillators, a sub, noise and ring modulation, two filters and two dirt stages, and every one of them has its own place left and right. A morph glides between two versions of every sound.

Try it in your browser, no FM-1 needed. Best with headphones: L/RRY lives in stereo.

0.1.0 beta: the firmware for the FM-1, the web editor and the web emulator are available now. This is a beta: tell us what you find.

The firmware has been run on one real FM-1 by its author. The way back to the official firmware has worked about three times, on the author's unit, and has not been tried on others. Installing is at your own risk: read the install guide and the recovery notes first.
