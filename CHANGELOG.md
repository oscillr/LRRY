# Changelog

## 0.1.0 beta (2026-11-13)

First public beta. L/RRY starts from DUELL's code and replaces its sound engine with a stereo voice.

### New
- The stereo voice: OSC 1 and OSC 2 (SAW, TRI, SINE, PULSE, SYNC, SUPER, FM, WAVEFOLD), SUB OSC, NOISE, RING, each with LEVEL, PAN and FILTER BALANCE.
- MULTI FILTER and TWIN FILTER with their own pans, SERIES or PARALLEL.
- DIRT A and DIRT B (SOFT, HARD, FOLD, FUZZ, CRUSH) with their own pans, SERIES or PARALLEL.
- MID and SIDE per patch.
- New matrix destinations: the pans of OSC 1, OSC 2, both filters, MID, SIDE and DIRT B DRIVE.
- The ALGORITHM knob moves MORPH on every page; MENU > CONTROL > ALGORITHM KNOB picks what it turns.
- The FX page: the patch's CHORUS, DELAY and REVERB send levels.
- GLO > MODULES > SENDS switches the effects off and gives their voices back.
- Both cores play voices, and the number on the screen is measured on the unit with everything on.
- HOME shows the sound as rings in the stereo field.
- 128 new patches.
- Web installer, web editor and a browser emulator.

### Not in L/RRY (from DUELL)
- The second part and the DUEL, STEREO and LAYER modes: L/RRY is one part.
- DUELL's ten engines, PERFORM, the master compressor, INSERT, EQ and DUCK.
