# L/RRY 0.1.0 beta

The first public beta of L/RRY, a new synth for the M-VAVE FM-1.

**Try it first in your browser:** <https://oscillr.github.io/LRRY/emu/>  
**Best with headphones:** L/RRY lives in stereo.  
**Install:** <https://oscillr.github.io/LRRY/install/> (Chrome or Edge, USB, back up first)

## What it is
- **A stereo voice.** Two oscillators with eight models each, a sub, noise and ring modulation, two filters and two dirt stages, and every one of them has its own pan.
- **Two filters.** The MULTI FILTER (LP, BP, HP, NOTCH) and the TWIN FILTER (LP, BP, HP, NOTCH, LADDER, COMB), in SERIES or PARALLEL, each source with its own balance between them.
- **Two dirt stages.** SOFT, HARD, FOLD, FUZZ and CRUSH, each with its own pan.
- **MORPH.** One fader glides between End A and a rolled End B. The ALGORITHM knob moves it on every page.
- **Movement everywhere.** AMP ENV, ENV 2, the CYCLE envelope, two LFOs and an 8-slot matrix that reaches every pan.
- **Effects.** Stereo chorus, delay with four modes, six reverbs. SENDS OFF gives their voices back.
- **Both cores play.** The screen always shows the real number of voices.
- **128 patches.**
